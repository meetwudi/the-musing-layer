# Musing Space

This folder holds musings about the musing space itself.

Two phrases anchor the direction:

- Remember the shape of my becoming.
- Help me stay in contact with what I am becoming.

The practical product lens is that this should become a clean MCP interface that can integrate with major AI platforms. The durable layer is not any one model or chat surface. The durable layer is memory, ontology, reflection, care, and inspectable sensemaking.

## Working Principles

- The system mirrors, not steers.
- The human remains the author of becoming.
- Memory should be inspectable, editable, reversible, and corrigible.
- Latent stewardship should be visible as observations, connections, and hypotheses, not unsolicited advice.
- The core ontology should be stable enough to support product behavior.
- The evolved ontology should remain flexible, but it needs mechanisms that prevent endless AI category growth.
